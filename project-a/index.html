What we know so far about Brain worm? It lives inside our brains and carries our memories and emotions. In the year 3099, Langton’s team first discovered it in a test subject’s brain. They found that stimulating it could make the subject relive specific memories or emotions. Langton hoped to use this ability to retrieve his own lost memories. Driven by desperation, he tested the procedure on himself. But the creature was enraged. Its overwhelming emotion took over his entire brain.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature It can be studied to explore memory and emotion. Researchers may capture it for a short time to run experiments, yet they must watch the time carefully — or they will be consumed by it. Langton left only one note for everyone: 500 matters.
